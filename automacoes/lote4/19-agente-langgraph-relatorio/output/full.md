# Research brief

**Question:** What is the market size, pricing and competition for coffee subscriptions, and what about churn?

_Model: mock-deterministic. Every claim carries a source id; the list is at the end._

## Market size and growth

Specialty coffee subscriptions reached US$ 2.4 billion in 2025, up 18% from 2024. [d01] A panel of 3,200 buyers found 61% subscribe for convenience and 27% for discovery of new roasters. [d02]

## Pricing

The median 250 g bag costs US$ 14.50 on subscription and US$ 16.90 on single purchase. [d03] Shipping a 250 g bag costs US$ 4.80 on average, 33% of the subscription price. [d07]

## Competition

Five regional roasters hold 38% of subscription revenue. [d04] Bean Harbor lowered its entry plan to US$ 12.90 in September 2025 and added a skip-a-month option. [d08]

## Retention and churn

Monthly churn is 9% in the first three months and 4% afterwards. [d05] Bean Harbor lowered its entry plan to US$ 12.90 in September 2025 and added a skip-a-month option. [d08]
