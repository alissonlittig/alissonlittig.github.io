# Warranty
All Brisa appliances include a 24-month limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects.
The air fryer and the blender motor have an extended 36-month warranty when registered within 30 days of purchase.
Damage from drops, water immersion of the base, or use of non-original parts is not covered.
To claim, open a ticket with the order number and a short video showing the problem. Support replies in one business day.
