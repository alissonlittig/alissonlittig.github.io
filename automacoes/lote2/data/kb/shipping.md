# Shipping
Orders placed before 2 PM on business days ship the same day. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days in the US.
Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs US$ 14.90. Orders above US$ 75 ship free with standard delivery.
International shipping is available to Canada and the UK in 7 to 12 business days; customs fees are paid by the buyer.
