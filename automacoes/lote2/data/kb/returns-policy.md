# Returns policy
Customers may return any unused appliance within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Opened small appliances are accepted within 14 days if the original packaging and all accessories are included.
Return shipping is free for defective items. For change-of-mind returns the customer pays shipping, about US$ 8.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the warehouse inspects the item.
