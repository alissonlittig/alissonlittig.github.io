# RAG 18 · Invoices and bank slips in PDF / Notas fiscais e boletos em PDF

> Study prototype, **not in production anywhere**. 34 invented PDFs (37 documents; companies, tax IDs and amounts are made up, CC0). Not accounting or tax advice. / Protótipo de estudo, **não está em produção em lugar nenhum**. PDFs inventados.

## EN
"How much did I pay for electricity in September?" → the sum, in cents, with the **file and page** of every document used.

```
make_pdfs.py -> 34 PDFs (invoices + bank slips; one PDF has 4 pages)
PDF -> pypdf text per page -> regex fields (issuer, date, amount, status) -> category by rules on the description
question -> issuer/category (hand-written entity list) + month(s) + paid/open -> filter -> SUM + BM25 score per document
          -> unknown word (e.g. "Netflix") = refuse, never "sum everything"
```
Rules: invoice = paid on issue; a paid bank slip counts in the month it was paid; an open one counts in its due month. The "approximate taxes" line in the invoice is ignored (tested).
**Measured:** extraction equals the answer key on 37/37 documents; 21 questions (totals and counts written by hand) 21/21 exact; out-of-scope 0/6 answered. **Optimistic:** the entity list and word rules were written looking at these questions.
**Real:** PDF text extraction, fields, filter, sum, refusal, browser demo (same question code as Python, parity test; PDFs open from the citation). **Simulated / limits:** PDFs are generated by me with a text layer (no OCR, scanned PDFs would not work); the browser uses the pre-extracted index; no language model; layouts are simple, a real invoice from another city would need new regexes.

Run: `python make_pdfs.py` (regenerates PDFs) · `python rag.py "Quanto devo de condomínio?"` · `python tests/test_rag_18.py` (10 groups; needs `pypdf`; Node optional, only for JS parity) · `python build_index.py` · demo: open `demo/index.html`.

## PT
"Quanto paguei de energia em setembro?" → a soma, em centavos, com **arquivo e página** de cada documento usado. O texto dos PDFs é extraído de verdade (pypdf), a pergunta vira filtro (emitente ou categoria, mês, pago ou em aberto) e uma palavra desconhecida (ex.: "Netflix") recusa em vez de somar tudo. Regras: nota fiscal = paga na emissão; boleto pago conta no mês do pagamento, o em aberto no mês do vencimento. **Medido:** extração igual ao gabarito em 37/37 documentos; 21 perguntas (totais e contagens escritos à mão) 21/21 exatas; fora do escopo 0/6 respondidas. **Otimista:** a lista de entidades e as regras foram escritas olhando essas perguntas. **Limites:** PDFs gerados por mim com camada de texto (sem OCR), layout simples, sem modelo de linguagem.
