    Builds

    Six paths: attributes by level, weapons, runes, gems and a guide video.

    Level numbers are our own rough suggestions; adapt to your pace and check current guides.

    Strength

    Attributes: Vitality and Strength first

    Heavy blows that break stance. For those who prefer impact over speed.

    The idea

    Break the enemy's stance with slow, heavy blows. You trade speed for impact: each clean hit takes far more than a quick blade's.

    How to play

    Hold the strong attack when the enemy whiffs, and open the guard with a shot before the heavy blow. Don't chain: one hit, back off, watch.

    Strengths

    High damage per hit; breaks the stance of beasts and big bosses; hammers and axes have generous reach.

    Weaknesses

    Slow against fast bosses; every attack costs stamina; a mistimed swing is punished.

    Skill

    Attributes: Vitality and Skill first

    Fast, precise attacks, pairs well with parry.

    The idea

    Attack often and fast, dodging at the last moment. The build rewards learning an enemy's rhythm and answering with short combos.

    How to play

    Stay close, dodge through the swing and return a short string. Use the shot to parry when the enemy winds up.

    Strengths

    Great mobility; long combos; excellent with parries and visceral attacks.

    Weaknesses

    Lower damage per hit; demands reading attacks; leaves little room for positioning mistakes.

    Bloodtinge

    Attributes: Vitality and Bloodtinge first

    Firearms and blood; strong with lots of quicksilver.

    The idea

    Turn blood and bullets into damage. The firearm stops being support and becomes the main tool while the blade covers close range.

    How to play

    Alternate shot and strike. Visceral attacks refund bullets with the Oedon Writhe rune; the Chikage's transformed form trades health for damage, so watch your HP.

    Strengths

    Reliable ranged damage; good against slow bosses and flyers; frequent visceral attacks.

    Weaknesses

    Burns through quicksilver bullets; low durability; relies on accurate shots.

    Arcane

    Attributes: Vitality and Arcane first

    Arcane damage and mystical tools; fragile but explosive.

    The idea

    Scale Arcane for mystical damage and strange tools. It is the most fragile and most explosive build, with weapons that bend the damage type.

    How to play

    Use each weapon's special forms (the Holy Moonlight Sword releases a projectile) and leave long fights early: low health charges dearly.

    Strengths

    Explosive damage; great against targets weak to arcane; strong mystical tools.

    Weaknesses

    Low health and resistance; some weapons need the DLC; arcane-resistant bosses call for a plan B.

    Quality

    Attributes: Strength and Skill in balance

    Splits points evenly; flexible across weapons.

    The idea

    Split Strength and Skill almost evenly. Nearly every trick weapon is usable, and you can swap weapons without respeccing.

    How to play

    Start with the Saw Cleaver and move to Ludwig's Holy Blade in the nightmares. Vary between extended and folded forms by distance.

    Strengths

    Versatile; good for a first run; no penalty for swapping weapons.

    Weaknesses

    Never the best at anything; damage per point is split across two attributes.

    Elemental (fire/bolt)

    Attributes: High Arcane

    Elemental gems swap physical damage; good vs beasts.

    The idea

    Swap part of your physical damage for fire or bolt through gems, gaining from Arcane. Pays off against beasts (fire) and bolt-weak enemies.

    How to play

    Swap gems per boss and finish with fire or bolt tools. Careful: high Arcane leaves health low.

    Strengths

    Picks the target's weakness; handles beasts quickly; lots of variation.

    Weaknesses

    Requires constant gem swaps; some bosses resist both elements.
