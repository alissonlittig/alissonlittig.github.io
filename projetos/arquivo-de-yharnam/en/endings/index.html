    Endings

    Three endings and what each choice costs.

    01

    Yharnam Sunrise

    HowAccept Gehrman's offer (Submit your life).

    Gehrman beheads you with his scythe. The hunter wakes at sunrise while the Doll prays by the grave. The first ending.

    02

    Honoring Wishes

    HowRefuse Gehrman's offer and defeat him, without having consumed three umbilical cords.

    After Gehrman, the Moon Presence approaches and takes the hunter. The Doll pushes you in a wheelchair: the hunt begins again.

    03

    Childhood's Beginning

    HowRefuse the offer, consume three umbilical cords (four exist), and defeat Gehrman and the Moon Presence.

    The hunter becomes an infant creature in the Doll's arms. The secret ending.

    There are four cords in the game, but three consumed before Gehrman dies are enough. After that, Honoring Wishes is locked out.
