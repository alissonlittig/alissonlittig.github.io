    Yharnam Codex · IX

    Attributes

    Levels, Insight, frenzy and how each point weighs.

    Level rises by spending Blood Echoes with a Messenger (the Doll in the Dream). Cost grows each level. Each point goes into one attribute; Insight is separate and works as a second risk bar.

    Attributes

    Vitality

    Sets HP. First to raise for survival; strong early gains, slower later.

    Endurance

    Sets stamina and resistances. Dodging and attacking cost stamina, so it shapes your pace.

    Strength

    Heavy-weapon damage and weapon requirements (axes, hammers).

    Skill

    Damage for nimble weapons and requirements for precise ones (blades, swords).

    Bloodtinge

    Power of firearms and weapons that spend Quicksilver Bullets.

    Arcane

    Arcane damage, elemental gem damage and use of mystical tools.

    Item Discovery

    Not purchasable: comes from runes and covenants; raises enemy item drops.

    Systems

    Insight

    Each point of Insight is inhuman knowledge. Gained from bosses, items like Madman's Knowledge. Lets you ring the summoning bell but invites larger foes and raises frenzy.

    Frenzy / Frenesi

    When frenzy resistance hits zero the hunter takes heavy damage and loses half max HP until cured. Some foes inflict it by proximity; Deep Sea runes help.

    Rally

    After being hit, striking quickly regains part of the lost HP. Aggression is rewarded.

    Ataque visceral

    Shooting to stagger the foe and then striking gives high damage.

    Escalas (soft caps)

    Each damage attribute has diminishing returns (known marks near 25 and 50). Verify on an up-to-date source before building.

    Origins

    Origins (Milquetoast, Lone Survivor, Troubled Childhood, Violent Past, Professional, Military Veteran, Noble Scion, Cruel Fate, Waste of Skin) only change starting points and level; you can reshape later.
