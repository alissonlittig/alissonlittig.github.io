    Sources & method

    Where each fact came from, and what still needs checking.

    How this codex is made

    All text is original. Facts (names, places, effects) were checked against cited sources, never copied. Data lives in our own JSON files, one per section.

    What still needs checking

    On 2026-11-01 we checked against the Fextralife wiki (and Fandom for attributes): runes, gems, attributes, bosses (location, optional status, weaknesses), endings, weapons (scaling, DLC), firearms, tools, characters and areas; errors such as the order of the endings and several boss locations were fixed. Not yet verified line by line: lore, timeline, short weapon descriptions and fight tips. Build levels and soft caps are approximate. Only one chalice glyph was confirmed. Weapon type is our own classification.

    All sources

    • Bloodborne (FromSoftware / SIE, 2015 + The Old Hunters): items, dialogue, scenery
    • Wikipedia: Bloodborne
    • Fextralife: Caryll Runes (checked facts only)

    • Fextralife: Blood Gems (checked facts only)
    • Fandom: Stats (checked facts only)
