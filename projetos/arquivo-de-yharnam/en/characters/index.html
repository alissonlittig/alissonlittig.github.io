    Yharnam Codex · IV

    Characters

    Who you meet in the streets, windows and nightmares.

    The Doll

    Dream's helper

    A porcelain figure who levels you up with Echoes. Her presence is the Dream's only kindness.

    Gehrman, the First Hunter

    Mentor and jailer

    The first hunter, bound to the Dream. He trains the player and, at the end, offers death as release from the cycle; refusing leads to the fight.

    Eileen the Crow

    Hunter of hunters

    Leads the Hunter of Hunters covenant and puts down corrupted hunters. Her quest concerns duty, honor and where the Hunt ends.

    Alfred

    Hunter and guide

    A hunter in the Cathedral Ward who leads the Executioners covenant and searches for an old companion.

    Djura

    Defender of the oppressed

    A retired hunter in Old Yharnam who refuses to serve the Church and protects the townsfolk from the hunt.

    Father Gascoigne

    Transformed hunter

    A hunter who beat beasts and fell to one. Leaves a daughter and a theme.

    Henryk

    Eileen's hunter

    A veteran Church hunter whose armor set is known for its bolt resistance.

    Valtr

    Leader of The League

    A hunter in the Forbidden Woods who leads The League covenant.

    Iosefka

    Physician

    Runs Iosefka's Clinic in Central Yharnam. Her fate depends on player choices.

    Arianna

    Pregnant woman

    Found in the Cathedral Ward, pregnant with an unusual child. Supplies the Blood of Arianna. A tragic figure.

    Lady Maria

    Gehrman's apprentice

    Hunter of the Astral Clocktower; stands for loyalty and loss.

    Master Willem

    Head of Byrgenwerth

    Head of Byrgenwerth. Chose isolation before the Great Ones.

    Micolash

    Byrgenwerth student

    Seeks direct contact with Great Ones; builds a nightmare in Mensis.

    Laurence

    First vicar

    First vicar; corrupted and bound in the Hunter's Nightmare.

    Ludwig

    First Workshop hunter

    First hunter of the Workshop. Consumed by the beast.

    Logarius

    Martyr

    A Church martyr who refuses death, fought at Forsaken Cainhurst Castle.

    Queen Annalise

    Queen of Cainhurst

    Queen of Cainhurst; figure of blood and power.

    Saint Adeline

    Saint

    A saint figure tied to a covenant quest.

    Patches

    Trickster

    A trickster who lures you into traps and later sells Chalice items.

    Yurie

    Huntress

    Widow and hunter tied to Byrgenwerth.

    Gilbert

    Hunter

    A sick outsider hunter found in Central Yharnam.

    Simon, the Harrowed

    DLC hunter

    A DLC survivor of the Nightmare.
