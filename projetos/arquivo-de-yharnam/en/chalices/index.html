    Chalices

    The optional labyrinths: types, rituals and glyphs.

    Chalice Dungeons are optional labyrinths built from Pthumerian heritage. You open one with a Chalice (Root or standard) and a ritual, using materials from bosses and areas. Each has layers, bosses, treasure and traps.

    Layers and series

    01

    Pthumeru

    The first tier; a learning test.

    02

    Lower Pthumeru

    Second tier.

    03

    Pthumeru Ihyll

    Third tier; enables the Cursed Root chalice.

    04

    Hintertomb / Lower Hintertomb

    Series for resistance runes.

    05

    Ailing Loran / Lower Ailing Loran

    Series for elemental runes and gems.

    06

    Isz / Great Isz

    End chalices for strong runes.

    07

    Defiled / Cursed

    More dangerous variants with the Cursed rite.

    Rituals

    Rituals combine a Chalice with materials (Ritual Blood, Blood Rock) and allow 'rites' like the Cursed rite, raising difficulty and reward.
