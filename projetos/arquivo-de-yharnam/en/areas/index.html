    III

    Yharnam Codex · III

    Areas

    From the Hunter's Dream to the Astral Clocktower: the whole map.

    Hunter's Dream

    A dream refuge with the Workshop, the garden and the Doll.

    Central Yharnam

    Opening streets with mobs, the Cleric Beast on the bridge and Gascoigne.

    Cathedral Ward

    A district tied to the Healing Church, with Vicar Amelia.

    Old Yharnam

    A burnt, abandoned district housing the Blood-Starved Beast and Djura. Optional, but tied to chalice access.

    Upper Cathedral Ward

    Heights where Ebrietas, a Great One, lives, and the Celestial Emissary's Lumenflower Gardens.

    Hemwick Charnel Lane

    A grave-digger and witch district with the Witch of Hemwick.

    Forbidden Woods

    Dense forest with the Shadow of Yharnam; leads to Byrgenwerth.

    Byrgenwerth

    Ruined college; Rom guards the forbidden knowledge.

    Yahar'gul, Unseen Village

    A hidden village seating a secret cult; The One Reborn waits in Advent Plaza, the required way to the Nightmare of Mensis.

    Nightmare Frontier

    Nightmare region home to Amygdala, reached by being abducted by a Lesser Amygdala.

    Nightmare of Mensis

    Nightmare built by Micolash; home of Mergo's Wet Nurse.

    Hypogean Gaol

    An underground gaol with Darkbeast Paarl, tied to Djura.

    Cainhurst Castle

    Castle of ice and blood of the Vilebloods; the Forsaken Cainhurst Castle on the roof holds Martyr Logarius.

    Lecture Building

    Study building of Byrgenwerth, on the way to Rom.

    The Old Hunters

    Fishing Hamlet

    DLC fishing village where Orphan of Kos waits.

    The Old Hunters

    Hunter's Nightmare

    Nightmare of the original hunters: Ludwig, Laurence.

    The Old Hunters

    Astral Clocktower

    Lady Maria's clocktower.

    Chalice Dungeons

    Procedural labyrinths of the Pthumeru ruins. See Chalices.

    The Old Hunters

    Research Hall

    Research building inside the DLC Hunter's Nightmare.
