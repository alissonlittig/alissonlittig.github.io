    Blood Gems

    Effects and shapes of the gems that go on the blade.

    Blood Gems are slotted into weapons at the Workshop. Sockets unlock at +1, +3 and +6. Gem shape (radial, triangular, waning, circular, droplet) decides which weapon it fits; droplet fits any socket. Installation is not permanent: swap freely. A gem can carry up to three effects, and Cursed gems add one random drawback.

    Shapes

    Radial

    Right-hand weapons.

    Triangular

    Right-hand weapons.

    Waning (minguante)

    Right-hand weapons.

    Circular

    Left-hand weapons (exception: Lost Chikage).

    Droplet (gota)

    Any socket.

    Effects

    Tempering

    Raises physical damage by percentage.

    Adept

    Raises blunt or thrust damage by percentage.

    Fire

    Fire damage.

    Bolt

    Bolt damage.

    Arcane

    Arcane damage.

    Bloodtinge

    Blood damage; rare, dropped by Bloodsucking Beasts and Labyrinth Ritekeepers.

    Nourishing

    Raises all damage.

    Beasthunter

    More damage vs beasts.

    Kinhunter

    More damage vs the Kin.

    Striking

    Stronger charge attacks.

    Radiant

    Reduces stamina cost.

    Lethal

    Boosts rally healing.

    Fool

    More physical damage at full HP.

    Poorman

    More physical damage near death.

    Pulsing

    Slowly regenerates HP.

    Dense

    Raises weapon durability.

    Murky

    Adds slow poison.

    Dirty

    Adds rapid poison.

    Heavy

    Improves Strength scaling.

    Sharp

    Improves Skill scaling.

    Cold

    Improves Arcane scaling.

    Warm

    Improves Bloodtinge scaling.

    Odd (Tempering/Fire/Bolt/Arcane/Bloodtinge)

    Adds flat damage rather than percentage.

    Elemental-damage gems swap physical damage for fire/bolt/arcane and scale with Arcane; scaling gems (Heavy, Sharp, Cold, Warm) change how much Strength, Skill, Arcane or Bloodtinge count.
