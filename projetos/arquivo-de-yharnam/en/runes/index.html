    Runes

    Caryll Runes by group, with effect and where to find them.

    To use a Caryll Rune you inscribe it at the Memory Altar in the Hunter's Dream, and you first need the Rune Workshop Tool. You get four slots: three for ordinary runes and one for a covenant rune. Runes of one family at different tiers stack with each other; a rune never stacks with itself.
