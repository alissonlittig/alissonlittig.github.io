    Yharnam Codex · II

    Timeline

    From the underground civilization to the Night of the Hunt, in rebuilt order.

    The game gives no dates; the order below is rebuilt from items, scenery and community reading [reading].

    1. Step 01

      The Pthumerians

      Ancient civilization that studies and worships the Great Ones and leaves underground labyrinths.

    2. Step 02

      Discovery of the old blood

      Explorers find in the labyrinths a blood that heals and transforms.

    3. Step 03

      Byrgenwerth

      Scholars, with Master Willem, investigate the occult and 'inner eyes'.

    4. Step 04

      The split

      Disagreement over contacting the Great Ones: some found the Healing Church, others stay with Willem.

    5. Step 05

      The Healing Church and the Workshop

      The Church offers blood as remedy; the Workshop trains hunters.

    6. Step 06

      The Beast Scourge

      The cure shows side effects. The Church tries to contain it.

    7. Step 07

      The Night of the Hunt

      The city loses control; hunters and the night blur.

    8. Step 08

      The hunter's arrival

      The protagonist enters Yharnam seeking a cure.

    9. Step 09

      The Dream and the end

      The hunt leads to Gehrman and the Moon Presence; three endings.
