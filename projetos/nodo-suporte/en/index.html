When something breaks

One ticket, four steps

  1. You tell us

    Through the form or WhatsApp, in one sentence. No protocol number to memorize.

  2. Triage

    A technician sorts it: urgent, normal or question. Urgent means nobody can work.

  3. Remote fix

    Most things get fixed remotely. If needed, we come to you.

  4. Record

    Every ticket becomes a line in the monthly report: what broke and what changed.
