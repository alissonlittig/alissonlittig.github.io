Privacy

How we use your data

This is a portfolio site with a fictional brand. Forms and tools run only in your browser.

What happens to what you type

  • The contact form sends nothing: on submit the site only shows a sample ticket number.
  • The check-up and calculator compute everything on your screen and do not store answers.

What stays in your browser

Two preferences in local storage: chosen language and "reduce motion". You can clear them in browser settings.

If this were a real business

We would use name, email and message only to answer the request, based on your consent (Brazilian LGPD, art. 7, I), and delete it on request.
