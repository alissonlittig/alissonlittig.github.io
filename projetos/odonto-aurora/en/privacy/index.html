Privacy

How we use your data

Example text for the demo. In a real clinic a legal officer reviews it before publishing.

What we collect

Name and phone, when you use the booking tool or the contact form.

What it is for

Only to confirm your appointment or answer your message. We do not use it for advertising or share it with third parties.

On this demo site

  • Nothing is sent: the booking tool and the form only show the result on screen.
  • The site stores only your language choice in your browser.
  • It uses no tracking cookies or analytics tools.

Your rights (LGPD)

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data at any time through the clinic contact. Health records follow their own confidentiality rules.
