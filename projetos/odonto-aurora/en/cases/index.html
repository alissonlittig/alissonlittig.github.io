Our commitment

What we say and what we do not

Dental advertising has rules from the professional council. We follow them.

We say

  • How each treatment works and how long it usually takes.
  • The risks and aftercare, before you decide.
  • Prices only after the assessment, in writing.

We do not say

  • That results are guaranteed.
  • Bait prices or promotions that rush the decision.
  • Testimonials without the patient’s written permission.

Questions

Questions that come in every week

Does it hurt?

We use local anesthesia whenever the procedure calls for it and wait for it to work before starting. You agree on a hand signal to pause at any time.

I am afraid of dentists. Now what?

Mention it when booking. The first visit becomes conversation and exam only, no treatment, and we explain every instrument before using it.

How much does it cost?

The price comes only after the assessment, in writing and with options. The simulator on this site uses example numbers so you can see how installments work.

Do you see children?

Yes, from the first tooth. The children room has a chair and lighting sized for them, and visits are shorter.

Do I need to bring x-rays?

If you have a recent x-ray, bring it. If not, we recommend and take whatever is needed at the first visit.

Can I reschedule?

Yes, with 24 hours notice on WhatsApp. That way the slot goes back to someone else.

Still have a question?

Ask at the first assessment or write through the contact form.
