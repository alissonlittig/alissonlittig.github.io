How we work

Three promises to whoever sits in the chair

  • Explain before touching

    Every procedure starts with what will happen, how long it takes and how to pause.

  • Quote in writing

    You take the options and prices home. Deciding calmly is part of the service.

  • Only what is needed

    No treatment that does not help. If waiting is better, we say so and book a follow-up.
