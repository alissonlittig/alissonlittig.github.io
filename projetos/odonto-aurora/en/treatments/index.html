Treatments

Five treatments, explained before we start

Each with duration, steps and aftercare. Diagnosis and quote come only after the visit.

Cleaning and prevention

Removes plaque and tartar, polishes, and teaches a better brushing routine.

Duration
40 to 50 minutes
Steps
1 visit
Note
Usually every 6 months; your dentist adjusts it to your case.
Book an assessment

Aftercare and what to expect

  • Mild sensitivity may last a day or two.
  • Daily flossing matters more than more frequent cleanings.
  • Tell us if your gums bleed when brushing: it is useful information.

Fillings

Treats a cavity under local anesthesia with tooth-colored resin.

Duration
40 to 60 minutes per tooth
Steps
Usually 1 visit per tooth
Note
Depends on how many cavities and where.
Book an assessment

Aftercare and what to expect

  • Avoid chewing on the treated side until the anesthesia wears off.
  • A high bite or lasting sensitivity: come back for an adjustment.
  • A new cavity next to a filling also needs checking.

Whitening

Supervised, after checking gums, cavities and existing fillings.

Duration
2 to 4 in-office visits, or 2 to 3 weeks with trays
Steps
After checking gums and fillings
Note
Results vary from person to person and tooth to tooth.
Book an assessment

Aftercare and what to expect

  • Sensitivity during treatment is common; tell us so we can adjust.
  • Fillings and veneers do not whiten.
  • Coffee, wine and smoking stain again over time.

Orthodontics

Fixed, ceramic or aligners: the assessment says which one fits your case.

Duration
12 to 36 months, with monthly adjustments
Steps
Assessment, records and fitting
Note
Fixed, ceramic or aligners: depends on the case and your preference.
Book an assessment

Aftercare and what to expect

  • Brush after every meal, with an interdental brush.
  • Missing adjustments lengthens the total time.
  • After braces, the retainer is part of the treatment.

Dental implants

Replaces a missing tooth, planned from imaging before surgery.

Duration
1 to 2 hour surgery; 3 to 6 months of healing
Steps
Assessment, imaging, surgery, crown
Note
Depends on available bone and gum health.
Book an assessment

Aftercare and what to expect

  • Smoking slows healing; we talk about it beforehand.
  • Cleaning around the implant matters as much as around your teeth.
  • Regular check-ups are part of the treatment.
