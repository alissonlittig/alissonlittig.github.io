Glossary

Five words you will hear

DAS
Single tax slip for Simples Nacional and MEI. Bundles several taxes into one monthly bill, due on the 20th.
Pró-labore
The salary of a partner who works in the company. It carries social security and differs from profit withdrawal.
Regime
How a company pays tax. MEI, Simples Nacional, presumed profit and real profit.
CNAE
Activity code of the company. It sets the Simples annex and what the business may do.
Fator R
Ratio of payroll to revenue. In some services it decides whether tax is cheaper.
