FAQ

Questions we get every week

Do you serve clients outside Curitiba?

We serve Curitiba and the region. Online works from anywhere in Paraná, but in-person meetings are in Curitiba only.

Can I switch accountants mid-year?

Yes. We ask for paid slips and filings already made and carry on from where it stopped.

My old accountant will not hand over documents. Now what?

Tell us. We explain how to ask in writing and give you a template.

How long does it take to open a company?

It depends on the type and the city. MEI is usually same day. A limited company takes longer.

What if I go over the MEI limit?

We warn you when revenue gets close and plan the move to ME in Simples before it becomes a problem.

Does the price change during the year?

Only if the company changes regime or team size. You are told in advance.

How do I pay?

Pix or bank slip, monthly or yearly.

What happens to my documents?

We keep only what the service needs. See the data page.
