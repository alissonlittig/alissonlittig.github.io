Privacy

How we use your data

Short text, no legalese.

This is a demo page of a fictional site. The contact form validates fields in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. In a real version, the text below would apply.

What data we ask for

Name, email, WhatsApp (optional), company type, preferred time and the message you write.

What we use it for

Only to answer your enquiry and propose a time to talk. We do not sell or share it for advertising.

Legal basis

Your consent, given by ticking the form box (Brazilian LGPD, art. 7, I).

For how long

Until the conversation ends. If you do not become a client, we delete it within 6 months.

What stays on your device

  • The language you chose (PT or EN).
  • Which documents you ticked on the How it works page.

This stays in your browser only and goes to no server. We use no tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion of your data at any time, at contato@lumecontabil.example (example email).
