About

Accounting with the lights on

Lume is an old Portuguese word for light. The idea is simple: you see what happens to your company.

Pinhão, the araucaria seed, next to a R$ 1 coin. Photo: Adrian198cm, public domain

The brand

A Curitiba tree and a small lamp

The araucaria is the symbol tree of Paraná and appears at the top of the site. The yellow dot is the light: it moves along the top bar as you scroll.

How we work

Four agreements

  • Plain language, not accountant-speak

    Every hard term comes with a sentence that explains it. If it is still unclear, the question is welcome.

  • Warn before it is due

    A late slip becomes a fine. That is why the reminder goes out early, not on the day.

  • One accountant in charge

    You do not retell the company's story with every message.

  • A price that fits on one line

    The plan price is what the table says. Tax stays separate and visible.
