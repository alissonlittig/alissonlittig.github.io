The place

A cove that fits in an afternoon

Praia da Mansa has 600 meters of sand and shallow water, good for kids and for anyone who just wants to float. Behind it, a short trail climbs to the headland.

In the village, a 6-minute walk, you will find the market, the bakery and six restaurants. You will not need a car once you arrive.
