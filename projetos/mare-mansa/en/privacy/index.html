Privacy

How we use your data

Sample text for the fictional Maré Mansa inn, written with Brazil's LGPD (Law 13,709/2018) in mind.

What happens when you use the site

The calendar, the total calculation, the itinerary builder and the room filter all run entirely in your browser. Dates, guest count and room are not sent to any server of this inn.

Purpose

To build a booking message that you send yourself. We do not collect name, phone or e-mail on this site.

The WhatsApp button

When you tap "Open in WhatsApp", the message text goes to your app. From there, handling follows WhatsApp's policy. If you prefer, use "Copy message".

What we keep on your device

Only two preferences, in the browser's local storage: language and reduced motion. No tracking cookies, no analytics, no ads.

Your rights

At a real inn, you could ask for access, correction and deletion of data through reception. Here nothing is collected, so there is nothing to erase.
