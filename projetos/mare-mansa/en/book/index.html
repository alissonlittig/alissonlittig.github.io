Getting here

By car to the village, the rest on foot

The paved road runs to the village. From there, a 400-meter street leads to the inn. The last 800 meters of road before the village are packed dirt, fine for a regular car.

No car? Arrange pickup at the nearest city bus station with reception, for a sample fee of R$ 80.

Questions

What people ask before booking

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 2 p.m. and check-out by 11 a.m. Arriving earlier? We store your luggage and you can use the beach.

How does payment work?

Sample rule: a 30% deposit by Pix to hold the dates and the rest on arrival, by Pix or card.

Can I cancel?

Sample rule: free cancellation up to 7 days before check-in. After that, the deposit stays as credit for 6 months.

Is there parking?

Ten spaces on the grounds, free. The last 800 meters of road are packed dirt.

Are pets allowed?

No. The cove has bird nests on the headland and we ask that pets stay home.

Is breakfast included?

Yes, from 7:30 to 10 a.m., on the porch. Tell us when booking if anyone has a dietary restriction.
