Commission

A canvas made for your wall

Tell me the size and the room’s palette. I will reply with a study on paper and a quote.

  1. Conversation

    You tell me the size, the room and the colours around it.

  2. Study on paper

    A gouache in proportional format reaches you before the canvas.

  3. 50% deposit

    Once the study is approved, the canvas begins. The rest is paid on delivery.

  4. Making

    6 to 10 weeks, with progress photos. Shipped in a wooden crate.
