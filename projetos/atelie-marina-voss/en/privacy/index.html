How we use your data

This is a demo site for a fictional brand. It has no server of its own, uses no tracking cookies, and does not send what you type anywhere.

Commission form. The fields only build the text of a message in your browser. You decide whether to open WhatsApp and send it. If you do, WhatsApp handles the data under its own policy.

Local storage. We keep in your browser only the “Reduce motion” choice, to remember it on your next visit.

Example data. The WhatsApp number, prices and works are examples. In a real version, the data controller would be the artist.
