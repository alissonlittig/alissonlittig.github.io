Short text for publicity

Marina Voss (Joinville, 1984) lives and works in Curitiba. She paints large on the studio floor and small on paper, in short series. Solo shows include Breath (Galeria Passagem, 2026), Tides (Espaço Cais, 2025) and Blocks (2022).
