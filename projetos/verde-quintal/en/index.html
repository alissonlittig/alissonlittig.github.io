Three rules that cover almost everything

  1. 1

    Finger in the soil

    Push a finger to the second knuckle. Dry, water. Damp, wait two more days.

  2. 2

    A pot with a hole

    Standing water rots roots. A cachepot without a hole is decoration only: lift the pot out to water.

  3. 3

    Light comes first

    A plant in the dark does not die at once, it thins out and drops leaves. Move it a little at a time.
