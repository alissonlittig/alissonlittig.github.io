Common questions

How is the plant packed?

Upright in a box with paper wedging, soil held by a cardboard disc and leaves tied with soft tape.

The plant arrived damaged. Now what?

Send a photo within 7 days. We swap it at no cost or refund you.

Can I swap for another species?

Yes, paying or receiving the difference, as long as the plant is alive and in its original pot.

Do you ship in cold weather?

We do, with a thermal wrap when the forecast drops below 8 degrees.
