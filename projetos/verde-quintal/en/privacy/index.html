Privacy

How we use your data

Sample text for the fictional shop.

What we collect

On the contact form: name, e-mail and message. In the bag: chosen plants, gift note and quantity, kept only in your browser (local storage) so the bag does not empty when you change page.

What we use it for

Replying to your message and building the order text. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

In this example

Because the shop is fictional, no data leaves your browser: the form and the bag are simulations.

Your rights (LGPD)

You can ask for access, correction or deletion of your data. To clear the bag, use the Empty button or clear site data in your browser.
