    Habilidades

    Texto oficial do jogo, em inglês.

    • Mini Turret

      Deploy a turret that shoots enemies, dealing spirit damage over time. The turret expires after a limited lifetime.Turrets deal reduced damage to troopers and objectives.

    • Medicinal Specter

      Deploy a spirit that provides healing to nearby allies.

    • Spectral Wall

      Cast a wall that applies slow on enemies it passes through. Erupt the wall to divide the terrain in half and damage nearby enemies. After casting, press or Ability 3 to erupt the wall early. Can be Destroyed with Melee Attacks

    • Heavy Barrage

      Unleashes a volley of rockets that home in on a targeted location. McGinnis is slowed and is still able to use stamina.

    Sobre o herói

    Texto oficial do jogo, em inglês.

    Maggie McGinnis always had a knack for building things. At 4 she was using Lincoln Logs to recreate architectural marvels. At 10 she built and rewired a custom light fixture for her parents' anniversary. At 15 she designed a machine that refined the souls of the dead...that last one got a little more attention than the Lincoln Logs.<br><br>Now the head of Fairfax Industries Military R&D department, McGinnis spends her time in the machine shop, not in a lab. She's not interested in theory, to her science is magic you can touch.

    Precise placement is everything for McGinnis, whether she's creating a killzone or splitting the enemy team. She can help keep her team running making them hard to dislodge when entrenched.
