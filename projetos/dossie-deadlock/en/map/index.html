Map and objectives

What is in the city and what each thing is for. Original text.

Note: the City Never Sleeps update moved the game to a New York map and changed neutrals and UI. Check details in game.

  • Lanes

    Three streets connect one team's base to the other. Most of the fight for souls and ground happens there, and picking who goes where is the first decision of a match.

  • Troopers

    Waves of soldiers that march down a lane on their own. Killing enemy troopers pays souls, the currency that buys items and levels you up; letting a wave pass is giving up money.

  • Guardians

    Defensive towers at the front of each lane, the first structures to fall in a push. Taking one opens room for troopers and for the team to push.

  • Walkers

    Larger, sturdier structures, one per lane, behind the guardians. Losing one is a real map disadvantage and brings the enemy closer to your base.

  • Patron

    The final target: the boss guarding each base. Taking down the other one ends the match, so the leading team tries to close it out before a comeback happens.

  • Mid Boss

    A powerful monster at the heart of the city that only falls to a full team. Whoever kills it gets a strong, temporary push bonus; it is the big contested spot of the mid game.

  • Soul Urn

    An object that appears on the map and must be carried to a drop-off point. The carrier is exposed, so it creates a predictable fight with a good soul payout for the team that delivers.

  • Neutral camps

    Groups of monsters between lanes, belonging to no side. Clearing one pays souls and sometimes extra perks; it is how you earn when your lane is bad. The new update added neutrals called Haunts.

  • Zip lines and movement

    Cables across the city carry the team quickly between regions. Mastering movement (cables, shortcuts, dashes) decides who reaches fights first.

  • Shop and items

    At your base you spend souls on weapon, vitality and spirit items. The shop was redesigned in City Never Sleeps; see the current catalog in this site's Items section.
