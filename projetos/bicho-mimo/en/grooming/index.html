Grooming

Book a bath in a minute.

Build the service, pick a free slot and copy the request. The price updates live with breed and extras.

Prices

Bath: neutral shampoo, drying and brushing. Hygienic trim: paws, belly and sanitary area. Moisturizing: R$ 20 at any size. Long coats add R$ 15 and double coats add R$ 20.

What happens during the bath

  • Skin and coat check before wetting.
  • Neutral shampoo; conditioner only on request or for long coats.
  • Low-heat blow dry.
  • Ear cleaning and nail trim included.
  • A message when your pet is ready.
