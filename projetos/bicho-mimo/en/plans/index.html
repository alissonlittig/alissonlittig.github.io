Questions

Can I pause the plan?

Yes, with 5 days notice before the next cycle.

What if a bath falls on a holiday?

We move it to the same week at no cost.

Does a long coat change the price?

On a plan the discount applies to the base bath. The coat surcharge is billed separately, as with single visits.
