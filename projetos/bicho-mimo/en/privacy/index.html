Privacy

How we use your data

This is a demo site. It has no server of its own, sends no forms and uses no tracking cookies or analytics.

What stays in your browser

  • Vaccine card: pet name, birth date and doses, in the browser’s local storage.
  • Shop order: products and quantities.
  • The reduce-motion preference.

None of this is sent to anyone. To erase it, use the card’s delete button or clear site data in your browser.

In a real shop

The contact form and order would go to the shop only to answer you, based on your consent, and you could ask for deletion at any time (Brazil’s LGPD, art. 18).
