Before you write

Common questions

Can I use my own barcode reader?

Yes. Any USB or Bluetooth reader that types like a keyboard works, with no driver.

What if the internet drops?

You keep selling. Sales are saved on the device and sync when the connection is back.

Can I import my current product list?

Yes, from a spreadsheet saved as CSV with name, price and barcode.

Does it issue tax invoices?

Not in this example. A real product would have to follow the tax rules of each country.

Is the trial real?

No. Tallyroom is fictional, so the form sends nothing.
