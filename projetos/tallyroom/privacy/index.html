How we use your data

Short version: this demo collects nothing.

What is stored

One value in your browser, called tr-lang, that remembers whether you chose English or Portuguese. It never leaves your device. There are no cookies and no analytics.

The contact form

It checks your answers in the page and shows a confirmation. It sends nothing to a server, so nothing is stored.

Third parties

Fonts and images are served from this site. No external scripts, no embedded players.

If this were a real service

A real version would state who controls the data, the purpose and legal basis for each use, how long it is kept, and how to ask for access, correction or deletion, as the Brazilian LGPD requires.
