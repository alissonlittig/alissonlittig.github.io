    What it does

    Built for the counter, not the back office

    Four jobs, one screen. Nothing to install on the till.

    01

    Scan and sell

    Any USB barcode reader works, or the phone camera. Split a sale between cash and card, or into instalments, in one tap.

    How selling works
    02

    A shelf count that moves by itself

    Each sale takes one off. A delivery adds them back. The number on screen is the number on the shelf.

    How the count stays right
    03

    A reorder list before the weekend

    Set a minimum per product. When the count falls to it, the item lands on a list with the amount to buy.

    04

    Close the day in a minute

    Cash, card and payouts add up on their own. If the drawer is off, you see by how much, before you lock up.
