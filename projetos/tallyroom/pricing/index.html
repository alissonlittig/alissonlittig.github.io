FAQ

Questions about the bill

Is there a setup fee?

No. You pay the monthly or yearly price and nothing else.

Do you charge per sale?

No. Sales and products are unlimited on both plans.

What happens to my data if I leave?

Export products, sales and counts as CSV at any time. They are yours.

Can I change plans?

Yes, any day. The change counts from the next billing period.

Is this a real product?

No. Tallyroom is a fictional brand made for a portfolio. Prices and features are examples.
