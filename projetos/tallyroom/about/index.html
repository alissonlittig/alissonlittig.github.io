The idea

Four bars and a strike make five.

The tally mark is the oldest stock system there is: a stroke for each item, a diagonal for every fifth. It works because anyone can read it at a glance. Tallyroom keeps that count for you and keeps it readable.

Tallyroom is a made-up company. Alisson Littig designed it as a portfolio exercise, so there is no real team, customer or store behind this page. What follows is what the brand would stand for.

Principles

Four rules for the product

01

Count once

After the first count, nobody should walk the shelves with a clipboard again. If they must, the product failed.

02

The counter comes first

Selling must be faster than writing it on paper. Anything that slows a sale goes to a back screen.

03

No surprises on the bill

One price per store. No fee per sale, no fee to set up, no fee to leave.

04

Your data leaves with you

Everything exports to CSV, in plain columns, on any day.
