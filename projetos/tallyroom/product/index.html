Product

The whole counter, one screen.

Selling, shelf count, reorders and closing. Four jobs in one place, with nothing to install on the till.

01 Sell

Three taps to a sale.

Scan the item, pick how it is paid, done. A queue of five should take less time than a coffee order.

Barcode, camera or search

A USB reader works as a keyboard, so there is no driver. No reader? The phone camera scans.

Split payments

Part cash, part card, or instalments up to 12 times. Each part lands in its own total.

Keeps selling offline

Sales are saved on the device and sent when the connection returns.

Photo: Johnnybam, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

02 Stock

The count is the shelf.

Every sale subtracts. Every delivery adds. When someone counts the shelf by hand, the two numbers should match, and when they do not, you see which product drifted.

Minimum per product

Coffee beans: reorder at 4. Cables: reorder at 3. Each product has its own line.

Receive by scanning

Scan the boxes as they arrive. A short or extra box shows before the driver leaves.

The reorder list

One list, grouped by supplier, with the amount to buy. Send it by message or print it.

Supplier: Northside Wholesale

AA batteries, 4 pack ...... buy 9
Coffee beans 250 g ...... buy 4

03 Close

Close the day in a minute.

The system knows the cash sales. You count the drawer. The difference shows before you lock up. Try it with your own numbers.

04 Reports

Three reports, no more.

A

Best sellers by week

What to keep near the till, and what to never run out of.

B

Slow stock

Products with no sale in 60 days, with the money tied up in them.

C

Margin per product

Cost against price, so a discount never turns into a loss by accident.

Hardware

Works with what is on the counter.

Barcode readers

Any USB or Bluetooth reader that types like a keyboard.

Receipt printers

58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers, or a PDF receipt by message.

Phone, tablet or old laptop

It runs in the browser. If it opens a web page, it can be a till.
