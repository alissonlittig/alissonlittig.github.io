How it works

From a box of products to the first sale in an afternoon.

Bring your products

Paste a spreadsheet with name, price and barcode, or type the first ten and add the rest as you sell. Nothing is locked: you can export it all back to CSV.

  • Columns it understands: name, price, cost, barcode, supplier
  • Missing barcode? Scan the item once and it is saved
  • Duplicates are flagged before they are saved

Count the shelf once

Walk the shop with a phone and scan each product, typing the number you see. This is the only full count you will ever do. After it, sales and deliveries keep it moving.

  • Set a minimum per product while you are there
  • Skip a shelf and come back, progress is saved
  • An hour is typical for a shop of 300 products
Shelf B2

Sell as you always did

Scan, take payment, hand over the receipt. Each sale takes one off the count without anyone doing anything extra.

  • Cash, card, split or instalments
  • Staff logins show who sold what
  • Cancel a sale and the count goes back up

Reorder from the list

When a count reaches its minimum, the product joins the reorder list with the amount that brings it back to full. Group by supplier, send, receive by scanning.

  • The amount to buy is shelf size minus current count
  • Delivery differences show before the driver leaves
  • Edit any amount before sending
